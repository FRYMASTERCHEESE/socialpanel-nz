Privacy policy

Draft template only. The operator must verify it against actual providers, data flows, retention rules and the New Zealand Privacy Act 2020 before launch.

Preview mode

Without a connected backend, this website stores optional favourite choices, colour theme, preview orders and preview support tickets in your own browser's local storage. Preview data is not sent to a marketing supplier or payment provider.

Connected account mode

If configured with Supabase, account email and authentication data are handled by the configured Supabase project; service orders may store account identifiers, public URLs, order quantities and status. Support messages may be stored in the project's database. The database schema restricts access by user and administrator role.

Analytics, cookies and third parties

This template does not install third-party tracking analytics or advertising tags. Connecting identity or payment providers may involve their privacy practices and cookies. The operator must identify these providers here before use.

Information rights and contact

The operator must provide a real privacy contact, retention timetable and a process to request correction, export or deletion before collecting live customer information.
